---
title: times (in prison and probation)
---

In services for staff, use the 24-hour clock, including in time input fields. For example, 14:00 not 2pm.

This is because:

- the 24-hour clock is widely used in prisons. Using the 12-hour clock in prison may cause confusion.
- probation uses a mix of 12-hour and 24-hour clock but either are understood and NDelius uses the 24-hour clock. This is particularly important for services like MPOP that join up with NDelius in their user journeys.


For public-facing content, including content aimed at people on probation, consider whether the 12-hour clock will be easier to understand (see the [GOV.UK style guide times entry](https://guidance.publishing.service.gov.uk/writing-to-gov-uk-standards/style-guides/a-to-z-style-guide/#t:~:text=Lower%20case.-,Times,-When%20writing%20times)).
