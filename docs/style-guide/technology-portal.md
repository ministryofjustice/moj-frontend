---
title: technology portal
---

The platform people use to request support with digital services. This is distinct from ServiceNow, which is the platform the service desk uses to manage support requests.

Use lower case. Unlike ServiceNow, which is the name of a third-party product, technology portal is primarily a description of the service rather than a formal product name.

Avoid using ServiceNow, unless there is a specific need to do so. User research suggests that users do not generally recognise the term.
