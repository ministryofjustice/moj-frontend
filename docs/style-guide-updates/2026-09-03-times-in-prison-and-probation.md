We have added a new entry for times in prison and probation, replacing the separate prison and probation entries.

Guidance for prison services is unchanged.

For probation services, we now advise using 24-hour clock for staff. This makes it consistent with prison services, and broader probation services like NDelius and MPOP.

For the public, including people on probation, we advise that 12-hour clock may be clearer.
