Write out in full on first use with the first word capitalised, followed by the abbreviation MPOP in brackets: Manage people on probation (MPOP).

Use MPOP for subsequent mentions. Do not use mpop, MPoP or Mpop.

MPOP is a case management system used by probation practitioners to manage people on probation.
